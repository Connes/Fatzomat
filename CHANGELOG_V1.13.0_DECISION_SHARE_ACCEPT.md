# v1.13.0 Decision Share Acceptance

- Updated the shared-decision dialog copy to: "Deine verbundene Person hat die Entscheidung für heute getroffen."
- Changed the share action button from "Schließen" to "Übernehmen".
- Accepting a shared decision now writes it into the recipient's personal Today plan and navigates directly to Heute.
- Recipe decisions are saved to the recipient's recipe collection before being assigned to Today so the personal Today recipe remains readable under the existing RLS model.
- "Entscheide Du" keeps its existing "Los geht's" flow and direct navigation to Heute.
